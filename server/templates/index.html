🎲 Dice Chat

One-off chat rooms with integrated dice rolling, for casual tabletop play.

Rolling dice

Type /roll followed by an expression:

  • /roll 2d6+3 — sum of two d6, plus 3
  • /roll d20adv — d20 with advantage (dis for disadvantage)
  • /roll 4d6kh3 — keep the 3 highest (kl/dh/dl too)
  • /roll 10d6! — exploding dice; r2/ro2 to reroll low faces
  • /roll 8d10>=7 — count successes; dF Fate dice, d% percentile

Share the room link to invite players — no accounts. Rooms become read-only after 14 days and are deleted after a year.
